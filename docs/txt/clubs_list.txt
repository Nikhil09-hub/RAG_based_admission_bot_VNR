VNRVJIET Student Clubs

This document contains the complete list of student clubs at VNRVJIET.

1. Art of Living – Promotes yoga, meditation, mindfulness, and personality development.
2. CANDLEVES – Organizes cultural, creative, and student engagement activities.
3. Nrithya Tarang – Classical dance club that promotes Indian classical dance forms.
4. Creative Arts Club – Encourages painting, sketching, crafts, digital art, and creative expression.
5. Crescendo – Official music club for singing, instrumental music, bands, and performances.
6. Dramatrix – Theatre and drama club that organizes stage plays and performing arts events.
7. Data Questers Club – Technical club focused on data science, analytics, AI, and programming.
8. Livewire – Official dance club representing VNRVJIET in competitions and cultural festivals.
9. Krithomedh – AI & Machine Learning student community conducting workshops, hackathons, and technical events.
10. N Army – Student volunteer club promoting leadership, discipline, and service activities.
11. NSS – National Service Scheme promoting community service and social responsibility.
12. Scintillate – Photography club that conducts photography workshops and event coverage.
13. Social Media Club (SMC) – Focuses on photography, videography, content creation, branding, and digital storytelling.
14. Stentorian – English literary club conducting debates, writing, MUNs, and literary events.
15. Vignana Jyothi Sahithi Vanam – Telugu literary and cultural club promoting Telugu language and literature.
16. VJ TEATRO – Short filmmaking and media production club.
17. VJ ARC – Architecture and design club promoting creativity and design thinking.
18. VNRSF (Student Force) – Student volunteer club organizing campus events and leadership initiatives.
19. La Culina – Culinary club for cooking, baking, and food-related activities.
20. Student Alumni Relationship Cell (SARC) – Connects students with alumni through mentorship and networking.
21. VJ Vibes – Official podcast club producing campus podcasts and interviews.
22. VJ Startups – Entrepreneurship club encouraging innovation, startups, and business ideas.
23. Turing Hut – Programming club conducting coding contests, workshops, and competitive programming sessions.

Students can ask about any individual club to get detailed information about its objectives, activities, achievements, and events.
