Category:
Professional Computing Student Chapter

Established:

Description:
The ACM Student Chapter at VNR Vignana Jyothi Institute of Engineering & Technology is an official international student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM). - Coding Contests
- Hackathons
- Panel Discussions
- Interactive Sessions
- Technical Talks
- Webinars
- Alumni Sessions
- Placement Talks
- Quizzes
- Convergence Events
