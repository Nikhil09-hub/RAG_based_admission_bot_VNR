VNRVJIET Professional Chapters

This document contains the complete list of professional chapters at VNRVJIET.

1. ACM (Association for Computing Machinery) – Conducts coding contests, hackathons, workshops, seminars, webinars, and technical events in computer science and emerging technologies.

2. ASME (American Society of Mechanical Engineers) – Promotes mechanical engineering through design competitions, technical workshops, guest lectures, and engineering projects.

3. CSI (Computer Society of India) – Organizes technical workshops, coding events, seminars, hackathons, industry interactions, and skill development programs in computer science.

4. GDSC (Google Developer Student Club) – Google's student developer community conducting workshops, hackathons, project-building sessions, and events on Android, Web, Cloud, AI, and other Google technologies.

5. ICI (Indian Concrete Institute) – Focuses on concrete technology, civil engineering practices, technical lectures, industrial visits, seminars, and research activities.

6. IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) – Organizes technical talks, workshops, competitions, conferences, and professional development activities in engineering and technology.

7. IE(I) (Institution of Engineers (India)) – Promotes engineering knowledge through guest lectures, workshops, project exhibitions, technical events, and the annual technical fest "EmeRgE".

8. IGBC (Indian Green Building Council) – Encourages sustainable construction, green building technologies, environmental awareness, energy-efficient practices, and workshops on sustainable development.

Students can ask about any professional chapter to get detailed information about its objectives, activities, workshops, competitions, achievements, and membership.
