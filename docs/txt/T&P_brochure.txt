------------------------------------------------------------

- Structured mentoring from 1st year to 4th year.
- High Intensity Training programs.

- Continuous monitoring and feedback.

------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6: STUDENT ELIGIBILITY FOR CAMPUS PLACEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Mandatory Conditions:

- Minimum 75% attendance in training classes.
- Registration in 3rd year (2nd semester).
- Submission of latest CV online.
- Submission of 5 passport-size photographs.
- Submission of required certificates in soft and hard copies.
- Follow dress code norms.

Placement Rules:

- Maximum 5 attempts in campus drives.
- Once selected, a student cannot attend further drives except Dream Company drives.
- After Dream Company selection, no further drives are allowed.
- If absent after confirming an interview, the student is removed from the eligible list.
