Established:
2017

Description:
Turing Hut is the official programming club of the CSE and CSBS departments at VNR Vignana Jyothi Institute of Engineering & Technology. The club provides a platform for students to strengthen their programming, competitive coding, and problem-solving skills through collaborative learning, technical events, and coding competitions.

Objectives:
- Promote competitive programming and problem-solving.
- Encourage peer learning and technical collaboration.
- Develop coding and algorithmic thinking skills.
- Prepare students for national and international programming competitions.

Activities:
- Competitive Programming Workshops
- Coding Contests
- Peer Learning Sessions
- Technical Speaker Sessions
- Programming Challenges
- Turing Cup (National Inter-College Coding Competition)
- Convergence Coding Competition
- Summer Practice Contests
- Project Halloween
- Newsletters on Technology and Opportunities

Who Can Join?
Students from the CSE and CSBS departments who are interested in programming, competitive coding, algorithms, and software development.
