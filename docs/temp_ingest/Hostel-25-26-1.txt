1. GENERAL INFORMATION
------------------------------------------------------------

Hostels are constructed as per Fire Safety and HMDA norms.

Location:
- Within 1 kilometer of the VNR VJIET campus
- Address: Vignana Jyothi Nagar, Pragathi Nagar, Nizampet (S.O),
  Hyderabad – 500090, Telangana, India
- College Website: https://www.vnrvjiet.ac.in

Occupancy:
- Boys Hostel Capacity: 1,200 students
- Girls Hostel Capacity: 1,200 students
- Actual occupancy may vary based on room allocation and availability.

Infrastructure:
- Four high-speed elevators
- Separate entry and exit for junior and senior students
- 24-hour power backup
- High-speed internet connection
- Biometric and facial-recognition attendance system

Room Facilities:
- Individual cot for each student
- Study table
- Study chair
- Dedicated storage cabin
